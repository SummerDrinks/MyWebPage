Research & Projects

My research journey began with RF amplifier circuit design and gradually led me to discover my passion for robotics. Below are the research experiences and projects that have shaped my path.

Intention-aware Assistive Feeding Robot
Xuan Zhang, Ye Yuan, Hailong Huang
AIMS Lab, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University — Research Assistant, Feb 2024–May 2026
paper / video

I implemented eye-to-hand visual servoing with an Intel RealSense camera, ROS, and MoveIt to track the user’s mouth and guide feeding motions, and developed a SMACH state machine to generate intention-aware responses.

Hand-Held Magnetic Field Generator Alignment System
Microrobotics Lab, University of Toronto — Research Intern, May 2025–Aug 2025
report / video

I independently developed a handheld alignment system for a magnetic gastrointestinal device. This dual hall-effect sensor array system uses trust-region optimization and a cosine loss function to improve tracking accuracy for two permanent magnets with large signal-strength disparity.
